1. Who can take part

  1. Christmas Vouchers are available to registered Samira Home Decor members. Only orders placed while signed in to a member account count.
  2. Vouchers are issued in the member's name and are not transferable.

2. Earning your voucher

  1. Vouchers are earned once per year. Your tier is based on the qualifying orders you place on our website between 1 January and 24 December (inclusive, Adelaide time).
  2. A qualifying order is a paid, dispatched order with at least $100 of home decor items (after discounts, excluding shipping). Gift cards, vouchers, gift hampers and other gift items, packaging and cleaning services do not count. Orders that are cancelled or fully refunded do not count.
  3. You need at least 3 qualifying orders in the year to earn a voucher. Your voucher value is set by the combined total of your qualifying orders:
    TierQualifying ordersCombined totalVoucher
    Silver3 or moreAny amount$50
    Sapphire3 or more$1,500+$100
    Gold3 or more$3,500+$200
    Platinum3 or more$5,000+$300
  4. Each member receives one voucher per year, at the highest tier reached. Tiers do not stack.
  5. Vouchers are emailed to your registered address by 26 December.
  6. If an order is returned after the voucher is issued and your total falls into a lower tier, we may adjust the voucher or deduct the difference from your refund.

3. Using your voucher

  1. Silver, Sapphire and Gold vouchers may be used on our website between 26 December and 31 January (inclusive, Adelaide time). Unused vouchers expire after 31 January.
  2. Platinum vouchers may be used at any time within 12 months of issue.
  3. One voucher per order. The order value must be equal to or greater than the voucher value. Any unused balance is forfeited.
  4. Vouchers have no cash value and cannot be refunded or combined with other promotional codes unless we say otherwise.

4. Exclusions

  1. Christmas Vouchers can only be used on home decor items. They cannot be used to purchase gift cards, other vouchers, gift hampers or other gift items, packaging, shipping or delivery fees, or cleaning services.
  2. Vouchers do not apply to items marked "excluded from promotions".

5. General

  1. We may refuse or cancel any voucher obtained through misuse, fraud or error, including orders split or placed to manipulate tiers.
  2. We may change or end this program at any time. Orders placed before a change will still count under the terms that applied when they were placed.
  3. These terms do not affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
