Planned design services

Explore the services we’re preparing for Adelaide homes, properties for sale and short-stay rentals.

Home Styling & Property Staging

For homeowners refreshing their space and sellers preparing a property for market. We plan to arrange furniture, artwork and accessories to highlight each room’s best features and prepare it for listing photos and inspections.

Vacation Rental Styling

For Airbnb, Stayz and holiday-home owners. We plan to help choose durable furnishings, organise guest amenities and create a comfortable space that photographs well.

Interior Decorating

For furnished rooms or new homes that need a cohesive look. We plan to help with furniture placement, textiles, artwork, decorative lighting and finishing touches.

Furniture & Décor Sourcing

For shoppers who want help finding pieces that fit their space and budget. We plan to put together a shortlist based on your measurements, preferred finishes and what you already own.

Colour Consultation

For anyone choosing colours for a room or several connected spaces. We plan to recommend a palette that works with your natural light, existing finishes and furniture.

What to expect when consultations open

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about your project

    When consultations open, share your suburb, the rooms you’d like to work on, your timing and budget. Photos, measurements or a floor plan will help us understand the space.

  2. Step 2

    Talk through the space

    We’ll discuss how you want the rooms to feel and function, which pieces you want to keep and where you’d like help with layout, finishes or furnishing.

  3. Step 3

    Review your proposed plan

    Depending on the service, we’ll outline our styling recommendations, furniture and homewares selections, and a quote for the proposed work.

Design House FAQs

Can I book a consultation now?

Consultations are temporarily paused. We’ll post an update on this page when bookings open.

Can Design House help with a renovation?

We plan to help with the furnishing and styling decisions around a renovation, such as layout, finishes and pieces to purchase. Tell us which decisions are already made and what you’re still working through.

What will my service include?

That depends on the property and the work you need. The proposed scope and quote will outline what’s included before you decide to proceed.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Bring room photos, measurements, any floor plans you have, inspiration images and a list of pieces you’d like to keep. A rough budget and target date will help guide the conversation.
