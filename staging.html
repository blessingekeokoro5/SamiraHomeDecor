The proposed plan can cover furniture placement, textiles and finishing touches, with sourcing and installation discussed as part of the scope.

Home styling & staging

For a home you live in, the focus will be on using your existing furniture, improving the layout and choosing pieces that bring the room together. For a property going to market, we’ll discuss the rooms to stage, your photography date and whether furniture hire or installation fits the project.

  • Advice on what to keep, move or add
  • A staging scope aligned to your listing date
  • Furniture hire and installation options where suitable

Vacation rental styling

Design House FAQs

Can I book a consultation now?

Consultations are temporarily paused. We’ll post an update on this page when bookings open.

Can Design House help with a renovation?

We plan to help with the furnishing and styling decisions around a renovation, such as layout, finishes and pieces to purchase. Tell us which decisions are already made and what you’re still working through.

What will my service include?

That depends on the property and the work you need. The proposed scope and quote will outline what’s included before you decide to proceed.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Bring room photos, measurements, any floor plans you have, inspiration images and a list of pieces you’d like to keep. A rough budget and target date will help guide the conversation.
