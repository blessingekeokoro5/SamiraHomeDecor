Australian location suggestions use the Delivery Area rows from Marcus Schappi's australian-postcodes dataset, snapshot 2026-05-27:
https://github.com/schappim/australian-postcodes

Only suburb, postcode and state are served to shoppers; no shopper address is sent to a third-party lookup service. Leading zeros are retained. The dataset is community maintained and suggestions are not street-address validation.

Proposed James Lane delivery tariff (AUD), pending owner publication approval:
- Each heavy unit: $200 below 50 kg, $250 at 50–99.99 kg, $300 at 100 kg or above.
- Each medium unit: $180 below 25 kg, $190 at 25–39.99 kg, $200 at 40 kg or above.
- Each unit additionally pays its location adjustment: Adelaide/Sydney/Central Coast/Newcastle/Melbourne/Brisbane/Gold Coast/Sunshine Coast metro $0; Canberra $50; Perth $150; Hobart $200; Darwin $250.
- A postcode qualifies as metro when its geocoded localities are within 60 km of a listed hub in that state. Regional destinations within 300 km add $100 plus $25 per started 100 km beyond 60 km, per bulky unit, on top of the hub adjustment. The most distant geocoded locality controls postcode-level pricing. Darwin City 0800 uses the Darwin hub coordinate.
- Postcodes without usable coordinates, remote postcodes, and specified offshore destinations require a confirmed delivery quote before payment.
- Small parcels use $9.95 handling plus $2.50 per kg of actual cart quantity, with the location adjustment once per parcel order. Remote parcel destinations require confirmation too.

These are proposed store charges, not verified James Lane freight quotations. The original Adelaide example is supplier $160 for one Ares TV unit; the proposed Samira charge is $300 for that 135 kg product. Each additional Ares costs another $300 in Adelaide. Existing Luxo and Koala delivery calculations retain their supplier-specific rules.
