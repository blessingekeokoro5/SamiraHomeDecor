# Luxo ready-to-ship Office collection

Checked 12 November 2026: Odile walnut 160 cm storage desk and seven chair listings (Darren white, Colin cocoa brown/warm ivory, Janine mocha brown/light beige, Calvin light beige/mist green). Four Aglaya/Aerona listings marked as preorders are excluded.

Each unit retains the approved profit floor: desk $2,000 and chairs $1,500 above the supplier listing price at member pricing. Regular pricing is higher. Percentage discounts and rewards vouchers exclude these fixed-price items.

Delivery uses Luxo's public product calculator at https://lapi.luxoliving.com.au/api/siss-shopify-ep/rate with supplier SKU, quantity and the shopper's suburb/state/postcode. No name, phone, email or street address is sent to this service. The product page is rechecked for available stock and preorder flags before a quote is accepted. A short 60-second stock cache avoids repeated lookups.

Office shipping is the greater of the combined supplier quote and the quantity-weighted single-unit quotes, plus the existing $50/unit allowance and $102 for every additional unit. An office/other-product mixed cart adds the extra item allowance in the shared freight calculator so the browser and payment server agree. Pickup rates are never treated as free delivery. Unsupported addresses, unavailable stock, excessive quantities and failed supplier responses require confirmation before payment.
